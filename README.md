## About Me

I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate researcher with a focus on computational biology, bioinformatics, and machine learning[cite: 2]. My work centers on leveraging bulk RNA-seq, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics to explore disease mechanisms, identify molecular biomarkers, and discover therapeutic targets[cite: 2].

Currently, my research investigates the mechanistic insights of gut microbiota-derived metabolites in cancer mitigation (specifically colorectal cancer) using network pharmacology, molecular dynamics simulations, and machine learning models[cite: 2]. I aim to integrate multi-omics data with AI-driven computational workflows to advance data-driven discoveries in precision medicine and oncology[cite: 2].
