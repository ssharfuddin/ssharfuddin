## About Me

I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate researcher with research experience in computational biology, bioinformatics, transcriptomics, systems biology, and computational drug discovery. My research integrates RNA-seq analysis, network pharmacology, molecular docking, and molecular dynamics simulations to investigate complex biomedical questions and disease mechanisms.
I am interested in interdisciplinary research that combines large-scale biological datasets, computational methods, and AI-driven approaches to identify molecular signatures, biomarkers, therapeutic targets, and disease mechanisms.

## Research Interests

* **Cancer Genomics & Gut Microbiota:** Investigating host-microbiome interactions, disease mechanisms, and therapeutic targets in cancer development and mitigation.
* **Single-Cell & Spatial Omics:** Single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, tissue niche analysis, and cellular heterogeneity.
* **Bulk Omics & Meta-Analysis:** Bulk RNA-seq, differential expression (DESeq2), GSEA, network pharmacology, and REM meta-analysis.
* **AI & Machine Learning in Biomedicine:** High-dimensional biomedical data analysis, predictive modeling, and AI-driven drug discovery.
* **Structural Bioinformatics & Drug Discovery:** Virtual screening, molecular docking, molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, and protein-ligand interaction analysis.
